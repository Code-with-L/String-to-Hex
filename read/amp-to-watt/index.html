Electrical conversion

Amps to Watts

Convert current (A) and voltage (V) to power (W).

Power depends on current and voltage (and power factor for AC).

Conversion formula

P(W) = I(A) × V(V) (DC)
P(W) = PF × I(A) × V(V) (AC)

Examples

1 A × 120 V = 120 W (DC/resistive)
2 A × 230 V = 460 W (PF 1)
5 A × 12 V = 60 W
Note Multiply amps by volts (and PF for AC) to get watts.
