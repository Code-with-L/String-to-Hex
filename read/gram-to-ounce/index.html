Grams to Ounces

Convert g to oz.

g and oz are units of mass/weight. 1 oz = 28.349523125 g.

Conversion formula

oz = g × 0.03527396195

Examples

1 g = 0.035274 oz
10 g = 0.35274 oz
100 g = 3.527396 oz
Note Multiply g by 0.03527396195 to get oz.
