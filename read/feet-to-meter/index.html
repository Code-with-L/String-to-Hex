Feet to Meters

Convert ft to m.

ft and m are units of length. 1 ft = 0.3048 m.

Conversion formula

m = ft × 0.3048

Examples

1 ft = 0.3048 m
10 ft = 3.048 m
100 ft = 30.48 m
Note Multiply ft by 0.3048 to get m.
