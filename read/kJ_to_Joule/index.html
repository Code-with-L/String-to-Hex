Energy conversion

kilojoule to joule

Convert kilojoule (kJ) to joule (J).

kilojoule and joule are units of energy.

Conversion formula

J = kJ × 1000

Examples

1 kJ = 1000 J
10 kJ = 10000 J
100 kJ = 100000 J
