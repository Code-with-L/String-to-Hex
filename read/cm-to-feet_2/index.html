centimeter to foot

Convert centimeter (cm) to foot (ft).

centimeter and foot are units of length.

Conversion formula

ft = cm × 0.03280839895

Examples

1 cm = 0.03280839895 ft
10 cm = 0.3280839895 ft
100 cm = 3.280839895 ft
