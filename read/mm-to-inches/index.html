Millimeters to Inches

Convert mm to in with the standard conversion factor and worked examples.

mm and in are units of length. 1 inch is exactly 25.4 mm.

Conversion formula

in = mm × 0.0393700787
mm = in × 25.4

Examples

1 mm = 0.03937 in
10 mm = 0.393701 in
100 mm = 3.937008 in
Note Multiply the value in mm by 0.0393700787 to get in.
