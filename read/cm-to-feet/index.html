Centimeters to Feet

Convert centimeters (cm) to feet (ft) using the exact relationship between metric and imperial length units.

A centimeter is 1/100 of a meter in SI. A foot is an imperial/US customary length unit equal to 12 inches, exactly 0.3048 meters (30.48 cm).

Conversion formula

1 cm = 1 / 30.48 ft ≈ 0.0328084 ft
d(ft) = d(cm) / 30.48

Examples

20 cm = 20 / 30.48 ft ≈ 0.656168 ft
50 cm = 50 / 30.48 ft ≈ 1.64042 ft
