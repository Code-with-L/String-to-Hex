Volts to Amps

Convert voltage and resistance/power to current.

Current can be found from voltage and resistance or from power.

Conversion formula

I = V / R
I = P / V

Examples

12 V / 6 Ω = 2 A
120 W / 120 V = 1 A
230 V / 46 Ω = 5 A
Note Use I = V/R or I = P/V depending on known values.
