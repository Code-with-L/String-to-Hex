Stones to Kilograms

Convert st to kg.

st and kg are units of mass/weight. 1 stone = 6.35029318 kg.

Conversion formula

kg = st × 6.35029318

Examples

1 st = 6.350293 kg
10 st = 63.502932 kg
100 st = 635.029318 kg
Note Multiply st by 6.35029318 to get kg.
