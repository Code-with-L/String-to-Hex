1 Amps to Watts

How to calculate power in watts for 1 amps at common voltages.

Power (watts) depends on current (amps), voltage, and for AC circuits the power factor. Use P = I × V for DC and P = PF × I × V for AC.

DC / Resistive examples

P = 1 A × 12 V = 12 W
P = 1 A × 120 V = 120 W
P = 1 A × 230 V = 230 W

AC with power factor 0.8

P = 0.8 × 1 A × 120 V = 96 W
P = 0.8 × 1 A × 230 V = 184 W
Note Use the Current / power calculator for other values.
