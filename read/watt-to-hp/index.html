Watts to Horsepower

Convert W to hp.

W and hp are units of power. 1 mechanical hp = 745.699872 W.

Conversion formula

hp = W × 0.00134102209

Examples

1 W = 0.001341 hp
10 W = 0.01341 hp
100 W = 0.134102 hp
Note Multiply W by 0.00134102209 to get hp.
