Frequency conversion

Megahertz to Gigahertz

Convert MHz to GHz.

1 GHz = 1000 MHz.

Conversion formula

GHz = MHz / 1000

Examples

1000 MHz = 1 GHz
2400 MHz = 2.4 GHz
500 MHz = 0.5 GHz
Note Divide megahertz by 1000 to get gigahertz.
