inch to yard

Convert inch (in) to yard (yd).

inch and yard are units of length.

Conversion formula

yd = in × 0.02777777778

Examples

1 in = 0.02777777778 yd
10 in = 0.2777777778 yd
100 in = 2.777777778 yd
Note Use the length converter for other values.
