How to Convert kilowatt to boiler horsepower

Convert kilowatt (kW) to boiler horsepower (bhp).

kilowatt and boiler horsepower are units of power.

Conversion formula

bhp = kW × 1.34102209

Examples

1 kW = 1.34102209 bhp
10 kW = 13.4102209 bhp
100 kW = 134.102209 bhp
Note Use the power converter for other values.
