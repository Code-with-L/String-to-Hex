kilogram to pound

Convert kilogram (kg) to pound (lb).

kilogram and pound are units of weight.

Conversion formula

lb = kg × 2.204622622

Examples

1 kg = 2.204622622 lb
10 kg = 22.04622622 lb
100 kg = 220.4622622 lb
