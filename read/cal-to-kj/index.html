thermochemical calorie to kilojoule

Convert thermochemical calorie (cal) to kilojoule (kJ).

thermochemical calorie and kilojoule are units of energy.

Conversion formula

kJ = cal × 0.004184

Examples

1 cal = 0.004184 kJ
10 cal = 0.04184 kJ
100 cal = 0.4184 kJ
