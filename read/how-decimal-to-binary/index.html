How to Convert Decimal to Binary

Convert decimal numbers to binary.

Divide the number by 2 repeatedly and read remainders in reverse order.

Conversion formula

Binary = remainders of successive division by 2 (reverse order)

Examples

10₁₀ = 1010₂
15₁₀ = 1111₂
255₁₀ = 11111111₂
Note Use repeated division by 2.
