Electrical conversion

Amps to Volts

Convert current and resistance/power to voltage.

Voltage can be found from current and resistance (Ohm’s law) or from power.

Conversion formula

V = I × R
V = P / I

Examples

2 A × 10 Ω = 20 V
120 W / 2 A = 60 V
5 A × 12 Ω = 60 V
Note Use V = I×R or V = P/I depending on known values.
