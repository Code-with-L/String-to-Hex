Amps to kVA

Convert amps to kVA given voltage and phase.

Apparent power depends on current, voltage, and phase configuration.

Conversion formula

kVA = (I × V) / 1000 (single-phase)
kVA = (√3 × I × V) / 1000 (three-phase)

Examples

43.478 A × 230 V / 1000 ≈ 10 kVA (1φ)
14.434 A × 1.732 × 400 V / 1000 ≈ 10 kVA (3φ)
10 A × 120 V / 1000 = 1.2 kVA (1φ)
Note Use the appropriate formula for single-phase or three-phase systems.
