Centimeters to Inches

Convert cm to in with the standard conversion factor and worked examples.

cm and in are units of length. 1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm.

Conversion formula

in = cm × 0.3937007874
cm = in × 2.54

Examples

1 cm = 0.393701 in
10 cm = 3.937008 in
100 cm = 39.370079 in
Note Multiply the value in cm by 0.3937007874 to get in.
