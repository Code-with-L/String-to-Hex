Watt-hours to Milliamp-hours

Convert Wh to mAh.

Wh and mAh relate through voltage. Requires voltage: mAh = (Wh × 1000) / V.

Conversion formula

mAh = (Wh × 1000) / V

Examples

10 Wh at 5 V = 2000 mAh
10 Wh at 3.7 V ≈ 2702.7 mAh
1 Wh at 12 V ≈ 83.333 mAh
Note Requires voltage: mAh = (Wh × 1000) / V.
