Electrical & Electronics

  • Ohm's law solver — Enter any two of V, I, R or P — the other two are solved at once.
  • Voltage divider — Leave one field empty — the solver computes it from the other three.
  • Power factor & three-phase kVA — kVA = V × A × phase ÷ 1000, PF = kW ÷ kVA, kvar = √(kVA² − kW²).
  • Three-phase power — P = √3 · V · I · PF for star, 3 · V · I · PF for delta.
  • Voltage drop — R = 2ρL ÷ A and Vd = I·R, with √3/2 for three-phase.
  • dBm ⇄ dBW ⇄ watts — P(W) = 10^((dBm − 30)/10) — logarithmic power.
  • Decibel (dB) — Logarithmic ratios: power uses 10·log₁₀, amplitude uses 20·log₁₀.
  • Electronvolt ⇄ joule — 1 eV = 1.602176565 × 10⁻¹⁹ J = 1 volt per elementary charge.
  • Power efficiency — Useful output as a share of input — the rest becomes heat, sound and motion.
  • Electricity bill & consumption — kWh = kW × hours, cost = kWh × tariff.
  • Photometry (lux / lumen / candela) — E = I / d², Φ = 2πI(1 − cos(θ/2)), E = Φ / A.
  • Room lighting calculator — Area, target lux, lumen method, fixture count and total wattage — for a real room.
  • Kirchhoff's laws (KVL / KCL) — Write the loop or node as one term per line. With a single unknown, it solves for you.
  • Coulomb's law — Electrostatic force between two point charges: F = k·q₁q₂/r².
  • Capacitor calculator — Reactance, charge, energy, and series or parallel combination — the maths of the component pages.
  • Inductor calculator — Reactance, stored energy, series or parallel combination, and the voltage spike V = L·dI/dt.
  • Battery capacity — Ah, mAh, Wh, coulombs and runtime — every battery conversion in one place.
  • Current ↔ power (amps, watts, VA) — Amps, watts, VA and kWh↔W — with real power factor for AC systems.
  • Resistor colour code — Digit · digit · multiplier · tolerance, read straight off the bands.
