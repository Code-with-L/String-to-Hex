RMS voltage V_rms

The DC value with the same heating effect as the AC waveform.

For a sine wave the RMS value is the peak divided by √2. This is why a 230 V supply is quoted as 230 V and not 325 V.

V_rms = V_max / √2 ≈ 0.707 · V_max measured in volt (V)
