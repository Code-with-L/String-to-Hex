Capacitor calculator

Reactance, charge, energy, and series or parallel combination — the maths of the component pages.

Inputs

  • Question
  • Frequency (Hz)
  • Capacitance (µF)
  • Voltage (V)
  • Charge (µC)
  • Relative permittivity εᵣ (plates)
  • Plate area (cm²)
  • Plate spacing (mm)
  • Capacitances to combine, µF separated by spaces

What it calculates

Reactance, charge, energy, and series or parallel combination — the maths of the component pages. Results appear instantly as you type, and the “show the working” panel lists every step so the answer can be checked by hand.

How to use it

  1. Open the calculator and fill in the input fields.
  2. The result updates as you type; the working is shown beneath the results.
  3. Copy the result, or share the URL — your inputs are stored in it.
