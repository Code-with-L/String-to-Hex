Common electronic components

ComponentSymbolUnitWhat it does
ResistorRohm (Ω)Limits current. Converts electrical energy to heat.
CapacitorCfarad (F)Stores charge in an electric field. Blocks DC, passes AC.
InductorLhenry (H)Stores energy in a magnetic field. Passes DC, blocks AC.
Diode——Allows current to flow in one direction only.
LED——A diode that emits light when forward-biased.
TransistorQ—Current-controlled switch. Amplifies or switches.
MOSFETQ—Voltage-controlled switch. Efficient for high current.
Fuse—ampere (A)Melts and opens the circuit when current exceeds its rating.
SwitchS—Opens or closes a path, usually under operator control.
DIP switchSW—A bank of tiny switches in one dual-in-line package. Fitted once rather than flipped every day, used for addressing and configuration on boards.
Solder bridgeJP0 ΩA deliberate solder dot joining two pads — a zero-cost jumper for variant selection. Remove it with solder wick to change configuration.
JumperJP0 ΩA removable link across adjacent pins on a header, configuring the board.
PotentiometerRVohm (Ω)A variable divider — a resistor with an adjustable tap.
Relay coil——Electromagnet that mechanically switches a separate contact set.
Lamp——Converts electrical energy to light and heat.
