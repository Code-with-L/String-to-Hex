Power factor & three-phase kVA

kVA = V × A × phase ÷ 1000, PF = kW ÷ kVA, kvar = √(kVA² − kW²).

Three-phase power

P = √3 · V · I · PF for star, 3 · V · I · PF for delta.

Voltage drop

R = 2ρL ÷ A and Vd = I·R, with √3/2 for three-phase.

dBm ⇄ dBW ⇄ watts

P(W) = 10^((dBm − 30)/10) — logarithmic power.

Decibel (dB)

Logarithmic ratios: power uses 10·log₁₀, amplitude uses 20·log₁₀.

Electronvolt ⇄ joule

1 eV = 1.602176565 × 10⁻¹⁹ J = 1 volt per elementary charge.

Power efficiency

Useful output as a share of input — the rest becomes heat, sound and motion.

Electricity bill & consumption

kWh = kW × hours, cost = kWh × tariff.

Photometry (lux / lumen / candela)

E = I / d², Φ = 2πI(1 − cos(θ/2)), E = Φ / A.
